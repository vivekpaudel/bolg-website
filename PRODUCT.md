# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers seeking thoughtful writing on web development, design, and technology. They arrive via search, social links, or direct visit — expecting a reading experience that respects their attention. The admin (site owner) uses a separate dashboard for content management.

## Product Purpose

A personal blog platform that makes reading feel like a curated publication, not a template. Long-form posts are the core content; the homepage surfaces them clearly and the individual post page delivers a comfortable, focused reading experience. Success = readers stay to finish an article, then come back.

## Brand Personality

Clean, confident, personal. The voice is authoritative but approachable — a skilled practitioner writing for peers, not lecturing. No corporate sheen, no trendy ornament. Every element earns its place.

## Anti-references

Over-designed portfolio sites: excessive animation, unreadable thin type, form-over-function layouts where decoration obscures the writing. The blog is about the words, not the wrapper.

## Design Principles

1. **Content first, always** — Typography and spacing serve reading, not decoration. The design should make the words feel important, not compete with them.
2. **Confident restraint** — Few elements, each deliberately chosen. White space is structural, not lazy. If it doesn't add clarity, it doesn't belong.
3. **Personal, not corporate** — This is one person's voice. The design should feel like a well-considered personal space, not a SaaS product in blog clothing.
4. **Craft visible in the details** — The quality shows in what's absent (no visual noise) and what's precise (spacing rhythm, type pairing, motion that serves understanding).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Full keyboard navigation. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Sufficient contrast in both light and dark modes. Semantic HTML throughout.
